Who we are

Al Safarah Media is a brand of DeMoore Entertainment, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KVK), Netherlands. Contact: alsafarahmedia@gmail.com.

Our tool: Al Safarah Curator

Al Safarah Curator is an internal software tool used only by Al Safarah Media. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It creates and manages music playlists on our own YouTube channel and our own Spotify account.

YouTube API Services

Al Safarah Curator uses YouTube API Services. By using it, we agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access: public information about music videos (video IDs, titles, channel names and view counts) and our own channel's playlists. We sign in only to our own Google account (OAuth) to create and edit playlists on our channel.

What we do not do: we do not collect, store or share personal data of other YouTube users, we do not access anyone else's account, and we do not sell any data.

Storage and deletion: stored YouTube data is kept on our private server, is refreshed at least every 30 days (in practice every 14 days), and is deleted when it is no longer needed or within 30 days of a deletion request.

Revoking access: access of Al Safarah Curator to a Google account can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page.
